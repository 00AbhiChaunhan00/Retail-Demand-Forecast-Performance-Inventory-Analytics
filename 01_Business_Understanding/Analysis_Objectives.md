# Analysis Objectives

## Primary Investigation

Evaluate the difference between demand forecasts and actual sales.

## Investigation 1 – Forecast Performance

- Calculate total actual units sold.
- Calculate total forecasted units.
- Measure WAPE, MAE and forecast bias.
- Determine the proportion of over-forecasted and under-forecasted records.
- Examine monthly forecast performance.

## Investigation 2 – Forecast Error Patterns

- Identify dates with relatively high forecast errors.
- Compare forecast errors across recorded product IDs.
- Compare forecast errors across recorded categories and regions.
- Examine the distribution and concentration of forecast errors.
- Investigate whether available variables are associated with forecast errors.

## Investigation 3 – Inventory vs Demand

- Compare inventory levels with actual units sold.
- Calculate inventory-demand gaps.
- Estimate inventory coverage using clearly defined assumptions.
- Identify records with relatively high inventory-demand gaps.
- Examine whether inventory patterns are associated with forecast errors.
