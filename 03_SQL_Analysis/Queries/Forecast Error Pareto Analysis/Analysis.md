Investigation 4 — Forecast Error Pareto Analysis

Business question:
-Which products contribute the most to the total forecast error?

Why are we doing this?
-Suppose we have 20 products. All products may have forecasting errors, but some products may contribute much more to the overall error than others.

We want to prioritise the products responsible for the largest share of forecast error.

This follows the Pareto principle (80/20 analysis).
